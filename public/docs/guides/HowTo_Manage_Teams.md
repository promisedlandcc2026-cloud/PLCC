# How-To Guide: Managing Ministry Teams
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**For:** Ministry Leaders, Admin Staff  
**Last Updated:** April 21, 2026

---

## What You'll Learn
This guide shows you how to create ministry teams, manage members, process applications, and coordinate volunteers using PLCC Connect.

---

## Quick Start

1. Create your ministry team
2. Add team members
3. Process applications
4. Assign roles and schedule

---

## Step 1: Creating a Ministry Team

### Navigate to Teams

1. Go to **People → Teams**
2. Click "+ Create New Team"

### Enter Team Information

**Basic Details:**
- **Team Name**: "Children's Ministry Team"
- **Department**: Select category (Children, Youth, Worship, etc.)
- **Description**: Brief overview of team purpose
- **Leader**: Assign team leader from members

**Example:**
```
Team Name: Children's Ministry Team
Department: Children's Ministry
Description: Dedicated volunteers serving our youngest members through Sunday School, nursery care, and special events.
Leader: Sarah Johnson
```

### Configure Team Settings

**Membership:**
- **Open Enrollment**: Anyone can join
- **Application Required**: Must apply and be approved
- **Invitation Only**: Leader adds members directly

**Requirements:**
- **Background Check**: Required/Not Required
- **Training**: Required/Not Required
- **Minimum Age**: Set age requirement
- **Time Commitment**: Expected hours per month

**Communication:**
- **Team Email**: team-email@awc.org
- **Notification Preferences**: How team receives updates
- **Chat Channel**: Enable team chat

**Save Team**

---

## Step 2: Adding Team Members

### Method 1: Direct Add (Invitation Only Teams)

**Steps:**
1. Open your team page
2. Click "Add Member"
3. Search for person by name
4. Select their role:
   - Leader
   - Co-Leader
   - Member
   - Volunteer
5. Set their status:
   - Active
   - Inactive
   - On Leave
6. Add start date
7. Click "Add to Team"

---

### Method 2: Process Applications (Application Required Teams)

**View Applications:**
1. Go to **People → Team Applications**
2. Filter by your team
3. See pending applications

**Review Application:**
1. Click on applicant's name
2. Review their information:
   - Personal details
   - Ministry interest
   - Experience
   - Availability
   - References
3. Check background check status (if required)
4. Read their responses to application questions

**Approve or Decline:**

**To Approve:**
1. Click "Approve Application"
2. Select role for new member
3. Set start date
4. Add welcome message
5. Click "Confirm Approval"
6. System sends acceptance email
7. Member is added to team

**To Decline:**
1. Click "Decline Application"
2. Select reason (optional)
3. Add personal message (recommended)
4. Click "Confirm Decline"
5. System sends notification
6. Application is archived

---

### Method 3: Open Enrollment (Self-Join)

**How It Works:**
1. Members see team in directory
2. They click "Join Team"
3. They're automatically added
4. Leader receives notification
5. Leader can remove if inappropriate

**Managing Self-Joins:**
- Review new members weekly
- Welcome them personally
- Assign to specific roles
- Provide orientation

## Step 3: Managing Team Members

### View Team Roster

**Navigate:**
1. Go to **People → Teams**
2. Click on your team name
3. View "Members" tab

**Roster Shows:**
- Member name and photo
- Role on team
- Status (Active/Inactive)
- Join date
- Contact information
- Attendance record

---

### Edit Member Information

**Update Member:**
1. Click on member's name
2. Click "Edit"
3. Change:
   - Role
   - Status
   - Notes
   - Availability
4. Save changes

---

### Remove Team Member

**Steps:**
1. Click on member's name
2. Click "Remove from Team"
3. Select reason:
   - Completed service
   - Moved to different team
   - No longer available
   - Other
4. Add notes (optional)
5. Confirm removal
6. System sends notification (optional)

---

### Track Member Activity

**View:**
- **Attendance**: Service participation
- **Tasks Completed**: Assigned duties
- **Training Status**: Required courses
- **Background Check**: Expiration date
- **Notes**: Leader observations

## Step 4: Organizing Team Structure

### Create Sub-Teams or Roles

**Example: Children's Ministry Structure**
```
Children's Ministry Team
├── Nursery Team
│   ├── Nursery Coordinator
│   ├── Nursery Volunteers (6)
├── Pre-K Team
│   ├── Pre-K Teacher
│   ├── Pre-K Assistants (4)
├── Elementary Team
│   ├── Elementary Coordinator
│   ├── Sunday School Teachers (3)
│   ├── Activity Leaders (2)
└── Check-In Team
    ├── Check-In Coordinator
    ├── Check-In Volunteers (4)
```

**Creating Sub-Teams:**
1. Click "Add Sub-Team"
2. Name the sub-team
3. Assign sub-team leader
4. Move members to sub-team
5. Set sub-team specific settings

---

### Assign Specific Roles

**Common Roles:**
- **Team Leader**: Overall responsibility
- **Co-Leader**: Assists leader
- **Coordinator**: Manages specific area
- **Teacher**: Leads instruction
- **Assistant**: Supports teacher
- **Volunteer**: General support
- **Substitute**: Backup coverage

**Assigning Roles:**
1. Click member's name
2. Select "Change Role"
3. Choose from dropdown
4. Add role description
5. Save

## Step 5: Scheduling and Assignments

### Create Service Schedule

**Navigate:**
1. Go to your team page
2. Click "Schedule" tab
3. Click "+ Create Schedule"

**Configure:**
- **Service Date**: Select date
- **Service Time**: Morning, evening, etc.
- **Positions Needed**: How many volunteers
- **Specific Roles**: Which roles needed

**Example Schedule:**
```
Sunday, April 28, 2026 - 10:00 AM Service

Nursery (2 volunteers needed)
├── Sarah Johnson ✓ Confirmed
├── Mike Davis ✓ Confirmed

Pre-K (2 volunteers needed)
├── Lisa Chen ✓ Confirmed
├── [OPEN] - Need volunteer

Check-In (2 volunteers needed)
├── Tom Wilson ✓ Confirmed
├── Amy Brown ? Pending response
```

---

### Assign Members to Schedule

**Method 1: Manual Assignment**
1. Click "Assign" next to position
2. Select member from team roster
3. System sends notification
4. Member confirms or declines

**Method 2: Request Volunteers**
1. Click "Request Volunteers"
2. System emails all available members
3. Members respond with availability
4. Leader assigns from responses

**Method 3: Rotation Schedule**
1. Set up rotation pattern
2. System auto-assigns based on pattern
3. Members receive advance notice
4. Can request substitutes if needed

---

### Handle Schedule Changes

**Member Cancels:**
1. System notifies leader
2. Leader finds substitute
3. Assigns substitute
4. Both members notified

**Add Last-Minute Volunteer:**
1. Click "Add to Schedule"
2. Select member
3. Send immediate notification

**View Schedule Conflicts:**
- System highlights members scheduled multiple times
- Shows members on leave
- Indicates training/background check issues

**Navigate:**
1. Go to your team page

3. Choose method:
   - Email
   - SMS

**Compose Message:**
- **Subject**: Clear, concise

- Entire team

- Quick questions and answers
- Schedule coordination
- Prayer requests

## Best Practices

### Recruitment
- [ ] Clearly communicate expectations
- [ ] Make application process simple
- [ ] Respond to applications within 48 hours
- [ ] Provide thorough orientation
- [ ] Pair new members with mentors

### Retention
- [ ] Recognize and appreciate volunteers
- [ ] Provide ongoing training
- [ ] Communicate regularly
- [ ] Address concerns promptly
- [ ] Celebrate team wins

### Scheduling
- [ ] Schedule 4-6 weeks in advance
- [ ] Respect member availability
- [ ] Provide easy substitute process
- [ ] Send reminders 1 week and 1 day before
- [ ] Thank members after service

### Communication
- [ ] Use appropriate channel for message type
- [ ] Don't over-communicate
- [ ] Be clear and concise
- [ ] Respond to questions quickly
- [ ] Keep team informed of changes

## Troubleshooting

### "Can't Add Member to Team"
**Check:**
- Member exists in system
- Member meets requirements (age, background check)
- Team isn't at capacity limit
- You have permission to add members

### "Applications Not Appearing"
**Check:**
- Team is set to "Application Required"
- Application form is published
- Filters aren't hiding applications
- Refresh the page

### "Schedule Notifications Not Sending"
**Check:**
- Member has valid email/phone
- Notification settings are enabled
- Member hasn't opted out
- System email is configured
