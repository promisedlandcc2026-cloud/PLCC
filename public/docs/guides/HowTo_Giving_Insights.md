# How-To Guide: Using Giving Insights
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**Target Audience:** Finance Team, Pastors, Admin Staff

---

## 1. Accessing the Dashboard
1. Log in to the **PLCC Canaan**.
2. From the main navigation, select **Giving**.
3. Click on **Giving Insights** from the sub-menu.

---

## 2. Navigating the Interface

### A. Time Horizon Filters
Located at the top right, this allows you to toggle the scope of the data:
- **Last 30 Days**: Best for immediate momentum checks.
- **Last 90 Days**: Useful for quarterly reporting.
- **This Year**: Tracks annual progress against budget goals.
- **All Time**: Shows the full historical growth of the ministry.

### B. The Performance Matrix (Quick Stats)
Check the four top cards for a 5-second health check:
- **Total Contributions**: Are we meeting our financial needs?
- **Unique Donors**: Is our community of givers growing?
- **Average Gift**: How are economic factors affecting individual stewardship?
- **Recurring Pledges**: What does our guaranteed support look like?

---

## 3. Interpreting the Data

### Daily Giving Trends (The Bar Chart)
This shows you the "pulse" of the ministry. 
- Look for spikes on Sundays/Mondays.
- **Action**: If you see a downward slope over several weeks, it may be time to share a "Mission Moment" testimony to re-engage the congregation.

### Fund Allocation (The Breakdown)
This shows where the money is actually going.
- **Action**: Use this during board meetings to demonstrate how the congregation's heart is aligned with specific missions or building projects.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting & Accuracy
- **Data Sync**: The Insights page updates in real-time. If you just entered a donation in the "Donation Box", it will appear here immediately.
- **Missing Data?**: Ensure all physical cash collections are recorded via the **Donation Box** and all digital gifts are successfully imported.

---

## 5. Pro Tip: Ask PLCC AI
If you are confused by a specific trend or need a summary for a meeting, look for the **"Ask PLCC AI"** button on the dashboard for a plain-English explanation of your latest financial patterns.
