# How to Create Your PLCC Canaan Account
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**For:** People creating an PLCC Canaan account for the first time  
**Time:** About 5–7 minutes

---

## Before you begin

Make sure you can open the inbox for the email address you plan to use.

**Important**
- Create account uses **email + password** (name and phone first).
- After signup, you choose **I’m new here** or **I’m already a member** — the next screens change.
- If no ministries apply, leave those choices blank and continue.
- You may stay **Pending** until a Super Admin assigns your role in Staff & Roles.

**Need help?** If a screen looks different, refresh once. If you still cannot continue, contact an PLCC administrator.

Public walkthrough (no login required): `/#/help/member-setup`

---

## What you will do

1. Open PLCC Canaan and choose the Members portal  
2. Create an account (name, phone, email, password)  
3. Choose New guest or Current member  
4. Finish that short path, review, and enter the Vault  

Already have an account? Use **Sign in** (email code or passkey) — this guide is for **Create account** only.

---

## Part 1 · Create your login

### 1. Open the PLCC website
Go to [sanctum-live-nine.vercel.app](https://sanctum-live-nine.vercel.app). In the top navigation, select **PLCC Canaan**.

![PLCC website home page](/docs/guides/member-setup/step-01.jpg)

### 2. Choose the Members portal
On the PLCC Canaan portal screen, select the **Members** card.

![PLCC Canaan portal selection](/docs/guides/member-setup/step-02.jpg)

### 3. Select Create account
On Member Access, choose **Create account** (not Sign in).

![Member access Create account](/docs/guides/member-setup/step-03.jpg)

### 4. Enter your name and phone
Fill in first name, last name, and phone, then continue.

### 5. Add email, password, and agree to terms
Enter your email, choose a password, accept the terms, and create your account. PLCC Canaan then opens the welcome setup wizard.

---

## Part 2 · Choose your path

### 6. Say if you are new or already a member
The first setup screen asks whether you are **new to PLCC** or **already part of the church**.

| Choice | What happens next |
|--------|-------------------|
| **I’m new here** | Short welcome questionnaire |
| **I’m already a member** | Confirm profile + ministry align |

---

## If you chose: I’m new here

1. **Preferred name** — Title (Mr/Ms/Pastor) is optional.  
2. **Best phone number** — So the welcome team can reach you.  
3. **How did you hear about PLCC?** — Sunday visit, family/friend, social, event, or other.  
4. **What are you hoping for?** — Select all that apply (worship, Bible study, kids/youth, baptism/membership class, serving, prayer).  
5. **Optional note** — Prayer request or anything we should know (skippable).  
6. **Review → Finish setup** — Then you enter the dashboard.

---

## If you chose: I’m already a member

1. **Confirm preferred name**  
2. **Phone + birthday** — Phone required; birthday optional (anniversary blessings).  
3. **Where do you serve?** — Multi-select ministries, or leave blank; optional “other ministry” text.  
4. **How long with PLCC?** — Under 1 year, 1–5 years, or 5+ years.  
5. **Review → Finish setup** — Staff will confirm your account if you are still Pending.

---

## After you finish

- You land on your member dashboard.  
- If access is limited, you are likely still **Pending** — ask a Super Admin to assign **Member** (or another role) in **Settings → Staff & Roles**.  
- Update profile and ministries anytime under **Settings**.

---

## Quick troubleshooting

| Problem | What to try |
|--------|-------------|
| I already have an account | Use **Sign in**, not Create account. |
| Picked the wrong path | Use **Go Back** to the first setup question and choose again. |
| No ministry applies | Leave ministry selections blank (current path) or skip the optional note (new path). |
| Need to change an answer | Use Go Back during setup, or Settings after. |
| Dashboard says I need access | Ask Super Admin to assign your role in Staff & Roles. |
