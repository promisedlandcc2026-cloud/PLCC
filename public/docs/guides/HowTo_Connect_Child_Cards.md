# How to Enable Connect Child Cards (One Click)
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**For:** Staff who help parents see kids’ check-in cards in PLCC Connect  
**Time:** About 1–2 minutes

---

## What this does

Parents use **PLCC Connect → My Family** to open each child’s **View Child Card & Code** (QR + desk code).

Household membership alone is **not** enough. Staff must turn on **Family card access** for the parent.

Use the **one-click** button on the **parent** — do not open every child one by one unless you need a special case.

---

## Before you begin

On the parent’s Family & Access tab, make sure:

1. Their **Connect / Canaan login** is linked and **verified**
2. They are in a **structured household** with the kids listed as children/dependents

If kids are missing from the household roster, add them first (Family & Access household steps), then come back.

---

## Steps (staff)

1. Open **Members** (`/members`)
2. Search and open the **parent** (the adult who signs into Connect) — not the child
3. Open the **Family** / **Family & Access** tab
4. Near the top, find **One-click Connect child cards**
5. Tap **Enable cards for all household kids**
6. Confirm when asked

That turns on **View Card**, **Check In**, and **Check Out** for every child/dependent in that household for this parent’s login. It also removes a mistaken “self” child-card link if one exists.

---

## Check that it worked

1. Ask the parent to open **PLCC Connect → My Family** (or refresh that page)
2. Under **Children & Dependents**, each real child should show **Family card access authorized** and **View Child Card & Code**
3. The parent’s own name should **not** appear under Children

---

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What to do instead |
| --- | --- |
| Working on a **child** record and hunting Step 4 toggles | Open the **parent**, use the one-click button |
| Only adding the parent as guardian of themselves | Revoke that self link; enable cards for the kids |
| Expecting Connect to show cards after household only | Household shows names; guardian **View Card** unlocks the button |
| Opening Step 4 first and getting lost in toggles | Ignore Step 4 for normal families — use one-click |

---

## When to use Step 4 (optional / special cases)

**Step 4 — Guardian access** is for rare cases: one child only, a non-parent guardian, or turning off Check In / Check Out for someone.

For a normal mom/dad with all household kids in Connect: use **Enable cards for all household kids**.
