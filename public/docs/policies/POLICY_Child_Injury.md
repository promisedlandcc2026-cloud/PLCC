# Policy: Child Injury & Incident Response
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**
**Department:** Children's Ministry / Events
**Policy Type:** Safety & Liability
**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standard procedure for responding to any injury, medical event, or safety incident involving a child while in the care of PLCC's Children's Ministry. It protects the child, the parents, the volunteers, and the church.

> **This policy applies to all events where children are checked in under PLCC supervision — Sunday services, VBS, special events, and off-site ministry activities.**

---

## 2. Immediate Response (First 5 Minutes)

### Step 1 — Assess the situation
- Determine if the child is in immediate danger or requires emergency medical attention
- Do not move a child who may have a head, neck, or spinal injury
- Remove other children from the area calmly if needed

### Step 2 — Call 911 if any of the following apply
- The child is unconscious or unresponsive
- The child is having difficulty breathing
- There is visible severe bleeding that cannot be controlled
- A bone may be broken
- A head injury occurred with a fall from height
- Any situation where you are uncertain — err on the side of caution and call

### Step 3 — Notify the Children's Ministry Lead immediately
- Locate the Children's Ministry Lead or the Event Supervisor on duty
- Do not leave the child unattended
- A second volunteer or staff member must stay with the child at all times

### Step 4 — Contact the parent or guardian
- Retrieve the parent's contact information from the check-in record in the Vault (`/#/events/kids-checkin`)
- The Ministry Lead or designated staff member must personally notify the parent — not a volunteer
- If the parent cannot be reached within 5 minutes during a medical emergency, proceed with calling 911

---

## 3. First Aid Authorization

- Only **trained and currently certified** first aid/CPR volunteers or staff may administer first aid
- The church maintains a first aid kit at every children's ministry station
- Volunteers may apply basic comfort measures (pressure to a minor cut, ice pack to a bump) while awaiting the Ministry Lead
- **Volunteers may NOT administer any medication, including over-the-counter drugs, under any circumstances**

---

## 4. Incident Report Requirement

An **Incident Report must be completed for every injury or safety event**, no matter how minor.

### Who fills it out
- The Children's Ministry Lead (or designated supervisor on duty)
- The report must be completed **before the end of the event** in which the incident occurred

### What to include
- Date, time, and location of incident
- Child's full name, age, and parent/guardian name
- Description of what happened (factual, not speculative)
- Witnesses present (names and roles)
- Injuries observed
- First aid or medical care provided
- Whether 911 was called and outcome
- Whether parents were notified and how
- Volunteer(s) present at the time of the incident

### Where to submit
- Incident reports are submitted to the **Lead Pastor and Church Administrator** within 24 hours
- A copy is retained in the church's physical records file
- For serious incidents: also notify the church's insurance carrier per policy terms

---

## 5. No-Photo Rule During an Incident

- **No photographs or videos may be taken of an injured child at any time**
- Staff and volunteers must not post about any incident on social media
- Any documentation (photos of the scene, equipment, etc.) is for internal incident reporting only and must be handled by the Ministry Lead or Administrator — not shared publicly

---

## 6. Medical Attention Escalation

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Minor cut, scrape, or bruise | First aid on site, notify parent at pickup, complete report |
| Head bump with no loss of consciousness | Ice, monitor, notify parent immediately, complete report |
| Loss of consciousness — any duration | Call 911 immediately, notify parent, complete report |
| Severe allergic reaction | Call 911, use EpiPen if child's profile indicates one on file and you are trained, notify parent, complete report |
| Seizure | Call 911, do not restrain child, time the seizure, notify parent, complete report |
| Suspected broken bone | Do not move child, call 911, notify parent, complete report |

---

## 7. Follow-Up Timeline

| Timeframe | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Before end of event | Incident report completed |
| Within 24 hours | Report submitted to Lead Pastor and Administrator |
| Within 48 hours | Follow-up call to parent to check on child's condition |
| Within 7 days | Ministry Lead reviews incident with Children's Ministry team to prevent recurrence |

---

## 8. Volunteer Protection

- Volunteers who follow this policy in good faith are acting under PLCC's coverage
- Do not admit fault or liability to parents on behalf of the church
- Direct all legal questions or threats to the Lead Pastor or designated church administrator
- Volunteers should not discuss incidents with other congregation members

---

## 9. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Children's Ministry Lead and Church Administrator. Updates take effect immediately upon publication in the Vault Playbook.
