# Policy: Photography & Media at PLCC Events
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**
**Department:** Administration / Media Team
**Policy Type:** Media, Privacy & Consent
**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes rules for how photographs and videos are taken, used, and shared at PLCC services and events. It protects the privacy of our congregation — especially children — and sets clear expectations for staff, volunteers, and the Media Team.

---

## 2. General Notice to Congregation Members

> **NOTICE: By attending PLCC services and events, you acknowledge that Promised Land Christian Center may photograph or record the event for ministry, outreach, and communication purposes.**

PLCC uses photographs and video for:
- Church website and social media ministry outreach
- Internal newsletters and bulletins
- Event recaps and promotional materials
- Archival and historical documentation

**This is considered implied consent** for adults and minors whose parents/guardians have provided written consent.

### How to Opt Out
If you or your family do not wish to be photographed at PLCC events:
1. Submit a written opt-out request to the Church Office
2. Email the Administrator or Secretary listed at the front desk
3. Your preference will be noted in PLCC Canaan member record and communicated to the Media Team
4. Opt-outs apply to all future events until you request otherwise in writing

---

## 3. Rules for Staff, Volunteers & Media Team

### 3A — Who May Take Official Photos
- Only **designated Media Team members** or staff authorized by the Lead Pastor may take official event photos for church publication
- Personal photos by attendees are allowed during general services — see Section 4 for restrictions

### 3B — Approval Before Posting
- All photos and videos for church social media, the website, or bulletins must be **reviewed and approved** by the designated Media Lead or Administrator before publishing
- Unapproved photos may not be posted to official church accounts by anyone

### 3C — Posting to Personal Accounts
- Staff and volunteers may share general crowd photos, worship moments, and ministry highlights on personal accounts as long as:
  - No identifiable minors appear in the image (without consent on file — see Section 5)
  - The post reflects the values and reputation of PLCC
  - Content is not taken during a private or sensitive ministry moment (counseling, altar call one-on-one, prayer for personal situations)
- Volunteers are encouraged to ask the Ministry Lead before posting anything uncertain

### 3D — What May NOT Be Posted
- Close-up photos of individuals without their knowledge or permission
- Images taken during children's ministry without explicit consent for each child shown
- Photos or videos of any incident, injury, emergency, or sensitive pastoral moment
- Content that could embarrass, harm, or misrepresent any PLCC member or staff

---

## 4. Children & Minors — Stricter Rules Apply

All rules for minors are stricter than those for adults.

### 4A — Written Consent Required
- **No photo or video of a child may be published on any PLCC platform without written consent from a parent or guardian on file**
- Consent is collected during the member onboarding process or Kids Check-In enrollment
- Consent covers: church website, social media, bulletins, and video streams
- Consent does NOT cover: personal social media of staff/volunteers, external publications, or commercial use

### 4B — Photography in Children's Ministry Areas
- **Photography in Children's Ministry rooms is restricted to authorized Media Team members only**
- Parents may photograph their own child during pickup but must refrain from photographing other children
- Volunteers may not take personal photos in children's ministry spaces

### 4C — Children Without Consent on File
- Children whose parents have not provided media consent must not appear identifiably in any published photo
- During group shots, if a no-consent child is present, the photo must be cropped, angled, or withheld from publication
- The Media Team maintains a list of no-consent children — check this list before publishing any children's ministry photos

---

## 5. Event Signage & Implied Consent

At designated events (special services, VBS, concerts, outreach events), PLCC will display visible notices at entrances stating that photography and recording are in progress.

> **By entering the event, attendees acknowledge they may be photographed or recorded for PLCC ministry purposes.**

This constitutes implied consent for general crowd/atmosphere shots — not for close-up, identifiable individual photos, which still require consent or opt-out status to be checked.

---

## 6. Live Streaming & Video Recording

- All live streams are considered public publications — the same consent and opt-out rules apply
- Camera operators must avoid focusing on individuals who have submitted opt-out requests
- Children's ministry areas are never broadcast on the public live stream without explicit written parental consent

---

## 7. Social Media Incidents

If a photo or video posted by anyone (staff, volunteer, or attendee) causes a privacy concern or complaint:
1. Remove the content immediately upon notification
2. Notify the Lead Pastor and Administrator
3. Reach out to the affected individual to apologize and confirm removal
4. Document the incident in writing for the administrative record

---

## 8. Media Archive & Retention

- All official PLCC photos and videos are stored in the church's secure media archive
- Media is not shared with third parties without explicit written approval from the Lead Pastor
- Archived media of identifiable children is retained only while consent remains on file — upon consent withdrawal, images are removed from publication and flagged in the archive

---

## 9. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually. Questions should be directed to the Lead Pastor, Administrator, or Media Team Lead.
