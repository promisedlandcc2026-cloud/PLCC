# Standard Operating Procedure: Meeting Minutes Workflow
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**Department:** Administration / Leadership  
**Effective Date:** April 21, 2026  
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Overview
The **Meeting Minutes Workflow** is PLCC's official procedure for documenting, distributing, and tracking acknowledgment of leadership meetings, board decisions, and ministry planning sessions. This process ensures organizational transparency, accountability, and compliance with governance requirements.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Proper meeting documentation creates an official record of decisions, protects the organization legally, and ensures all stakeholders are informed and aligned.

---

## 2. Primary Purposes

### A. Official Record Keeping
Create permanent documentation of meetings:
- **Decision Documentation**: Record all votes and decisions
- **Action Items**: Track assignments and deadlines
- **Discussion Summary**: Capture key points and context
- **Attendance Tracking**: Document who was present

### B. Accountability & Compliance
Ensure organizational governance:
- **Signature Collection**: Verify members reviewed minutes
- **Version Control**: Track document revisions
- **Deadline Enforcement**: Ensure timely acknowledgment
- **Audit Trail**: Complete history of all changes

### C. Communication & Alignment
Keep leadership informed and aligned:
- **Distribution**: Ensure all stakeholders receive minutes
- **Acknowledgment Tracking**: Verify receipt and review
- **Follow-Up**: Remind non-responders
- **Accessibility**: Provide easy access to historical minutes

---

## 3. Meeting Minutes Process (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Meeting Preparation
**Staff Action:**
1. Before meeting, prepare:
   - Agenda template
   - Attendance sheet
   - Previous minutes for reference
   - Recording device (if used)
2. Assign minute-taker:
   - Secretary or designated staff
   - Backup person identified
3. Set up meeting space with:
   - Laptop for real-time notes
   - Projector for agenda display

**What Happens:**
- Meeting is properly documented from start
- All materials are ready
- Roles are clearly assigned

---

### Step 2: During Meeting - Take Notes
**Staff Action:**
1. Record meeting details:
   - Date, time, location
   - Attendees present
   - Attendees absent
   - Guests or visitors
2. Document agenda items:
   - Topic discussed
   - Key points raised
   - Decisions made
   - Votes taken (with counts)
   - Action items assigned
3. Note important quotes or statements
4. Track time for each agenda item

**What Happens:**
- Complete record is created in real-time
- Nothing important is missed
- Context is preserved

---

### Step 3: Draft Minutes Document
**Staff Action:**
1. Within 24 hours of meeting, create formal minutes:
   - Use standard PLCC template
   - Organize by agenda items
   - Format professionally
2. Include required sections:
   - **Header**: Meeting title, date, time, location
   - **Attendance**: Present, absent, guests
   - **Call to Order**: Opening time and person
   - **Approval of Previous Minutes**: Vote result
   - **Reports**: Financial, ministry, pastoral
   - **Old Business**: Follow-up items
   - **New Business**: New topics and decisions
   - **Action Items**: Assignments with deadlines
   - **Adjournment**: Closing time
3. Proofread for accuracy and clarity
4. Save as draft version

**What Happens:**
- Raw notes become formal document
- Standard format is maintained
- Document is ready for review

---

### Step 4: Create Minutes in System
**Staff Action:**
1. Navigate to **Communication → Minutes Manager**
2. Click "Create New Minutes"
3. Enter document details:
   - **Title**: "Board Meeting - April 2026"
   - **Meeting Date**: Select date
   - **Department**: Board, Leadership, Ministry, etc.
   - **Summary**: Brief 1-2 sentence overview
4. Upload or paste content:
   - Copy formatted text into editor
   - OR upload PDF/Word document
5. Set signature requirements:
   - **Require Signature**: Yes/No
   - **Deadline**: Date by which signatures needed
6. Save as draft

**What Happens:**
- Minutes are entered into PLCC Connect
- Document is stored in database
- Signature tracking is configured

---

### Step 5: Review and Approval
**Staff Action:**
1. Send draft to meeting chair for review:
   - Pastor or board president
   - Ministry leader
2. Chair reviews for:
   - Accuracy of decisions
   - Completeness of action items
   - Appropriate level of detail
3. Make any requested revisions
4. Get final approval from chair
5. Update document in system

**What Happens:**
- Leadership verifies accuracy
- Corrections are made
- Document is approved for distribution

---

### Step 6: Publish and Distribute
**Staff Action:**
1. In Minutes Manager, change status to "Published"
2. Select recipients:
   - All meeting attendees (required)
   - Relevant stakeholders
   - Department members
3. System automatically sends notification:
   - Email with link to minutes
   - In-app notification
   - Deadline reminder
4. Document becomes visible in:
   - **Communication → Minutes View**
   - Dashboard widget
   - Member portal

**What Happens:**
- Minutes are officially published
- All stakeholders are notified
- Acknowledgment tracking begins

---

### Step 7: Track Acknowledgments
**Staff Action:**
1. Monitor signature collection:
   - Navigate to **Minutes Manager**
   - View acknowledgment status
   - See who has/hasn't signed
2. Review signature details:
   - Timestamp of signature
   - IP address (for security)
   - User agent (device used)
3. Send reminders to non-responders:
   - Automated reminders at 3 days before deadline
   - Manual follow-up if needed
4. Track completion percentage

**What Happens:**
- System tracks who has acknowledged
- Reminders are sent automatically
- Compliance is monitored

---

### Step 8: Deadline Enforcement
**Staff Action:**
1. As deadline approaches:
   - Review outstanding signatures
   - Send final reminder 24 hours before
   - Contact non-responders directly
2. On deadline day:
   - Generate compliance report
   - Note any non-compliance
   - Escalate to leadership if needed
3. After deadline:
   - Close signature collection
   - Document final status
   - Report to meeting chair

**What Happens:**
- Deadline is enforced
- Non-compliance is documented
- Leadership is informed

---

### Step 9: Archive and File
**Staff Action:**
1. Once all signatures collected (or deadline passed):
   - Mark document as "Complete"
   - Generate final PDF with signatures
   - Download for permanent records
2. File in appropriate location:
   - Digital: Church file server
   - Physical: Printed copy in binder
3. Update meeting index:
   - Add to chronological list
   - Tag by department and year
4. Ensure accessibility for future reference

**What Happens:**
- Minutes are permanently archived
- Easy retrieval for future needs
- Compliance requirements met

## 4. Special Scenarios

### A. Confidential or Executive Session Minutes
**Process:**
1. Create separate minutes document for executive session
2. Mark as "Confidential" in system
3. Restrict distribution to:
   - Only those present in executive session
   - Pastor and board president
4. Do NOT include in general minutes
5. Store in secure location
6. Limit access in system to authorized roles

### B. Minutes Requiring Revision After Publication
**Process:**
1. Create new version of document
2. Note revision reason and date
3. Increment version number (v1.0 → v1.1)
4. Link to original version
5. Re-publish with "REVISED" notation
6. Notify all original recipients
7. Collect new acknowledgments if needed
8. Keep both versions in system

### C. Emergency or Special Called Meetings
**Process:**
1. Follow same process but note "Special Meeting"
2. Include reason for special meeting in header
3. Expedite distribution (within 12 hours)
4. Shorter acknowledgment deadline (3 days)
5. Ensure quorum is documented
6. Note any emergency actions taken

### D. Virtual/Hybrid Meeting Minutes
**Process:**
1. Note meeting format (Virtual, Hybrid, In-Person)
2. List virtual attendees separately
3. Document any technical issues
4. Include recording link if meeting was recorded
5. Note any votes taken via poll/chat
6. Verify virtual attendees' participation

## 6. Quality Standards

> [!WARNING]
> **Minutes are legal documents. Accuracy and completeness are critical.**

### Critical Requirements:
1. **Timeliness**: Draft within 24 hours, publish within 72 hours
2. **Accuracy**: All decisions and votes must be recorded correctly
3. **Completeness**: Include all required sections
4. **Objectivity**: Record facts, not opinions or interpretations
5. **Clarity**: Use clear, professional language
6. **Confidentiality**: Protect sensitive information appropriately
7. **Accessibility**: Ensure all stakeholders can access minutes

---

## 7. Content Guidelines

### What to Include:
- All motions made and vote results
- Action items with assigned person and deadline
- Financial reports and approvals
- Policy changes or new policies
- Major decisions and rationale
- Attendance and quorum verification

### What to Exclude:
- Verbatim transcripts (unless required)
- Personal opinions or commentary
- Off-topic discussions
- Confidential personnel matters (in general minutes)
- Inflammatory or sensitive statements
- Unnecessary detail that obscures key points

## 8. Troubleshooting

### Issue: Member Cannot Access Minutes
**Solution:**
- Verify member's role has permission
- Check that minutes were published (not draft)
- Confirm member is in recipient list
- Send direct link via email
- Reset member's password if login issue

### Issue: Signature Not Recording
**Solution:**
- Verify member is using supported browser
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try different device
- Contact IT if problem persists
- Manual signature collection as backup

### Issue: Deadline Passed with Low Compliance
**Solution:**
- Generate non-compliance report
- Contact non-responders individually
- Extend deadline if appropriate
- Document reason for non-compliance
- Report to leadership
- Consider policy enforcement

---

## 9. Legal & Compliance Notes

### Important Considerations:
1. **Retention**: Minutes must be kept permanently
2. **Amendments**: Any changes must be documented and approved
3. **Signatures**: Electronic signatures are legally binding
4. **Access**: Board minutes may be subject to member review
5. **Confidentiality**: Executive session minutes have special protections
6. **Audit**: Minutes may be requested during audits or legal proceedings
