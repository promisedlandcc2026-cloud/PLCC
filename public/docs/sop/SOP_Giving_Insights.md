# Standard Operating Procedure: Giving Insights Module
**Promised Land Christian Center (PLCC)**  
**Department:** Finance / Leadership  
**Effective Date:** March 15, 2026  
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Overview
The **Giving Insights** page serves as the financial command center for Promised Land Christian Center. While the **Transactions** page functions as the ministry's detailed receipt ledger, the **Insights Dashboard** provides leadership with a high-level overview of the church’s financial health and giving trends.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This dashboard allows church leadership to make informed, prayerful, and strategic decisions based on real data rather than assumptions.

---

## 2. Primary Purposes

### A. Financial Health Monitoring
Provide an instant snapshot of church financial activity to evaluate goals without reviewing individual entries:
- **Total Contributions**: Collective volume of giving for the selected period.
- **Unique Donors**: Number of individual stewards contributing.
- **Recurring Pledges**: Volume of committed, predictable support.
- **Average Gift Size**: The typical size of a single stewardship act.

### B. Trend Analysis
Track month-to-month patterns to identify shifts in congregational engagement:
- **Engagement Shifts**: For example, an increase in unique donors with flat total volume indicates broader participation with smaller individual gifts.
- **Proactive Response**: Early identification of decreasing contributions allows leadership to address stewardship teaching before critical budget impacts occur.

### C. Strategic Planning
Analyze the **Fund Allocation** to align future initiatives with the heart of the congregation:
- **Major Funds**: Missions, Building Fund, Youth, Outreach, and General Operations.
- **Vision Alignment**: Directing resources where the congregation feels most called to invest.

### D. Financial Predictability
Leverage **Recurring Pledges** for responsible long-term planning:
- Future-proof budget planning and program development.
- Confidently schedule infrastructure improvements and large-scale outreach.

---

## 3. Leadership Responsibility

To maintain financial integrity and strategic direction, the following review schedule is mandated:

| Frequency | Responsible Party | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly** | Finance Administrators | Monitor for anomalies and update records. |
| **Monthly** | Executive Leadership | Review trends for monthly ministry planning. |
| **Quarterly** | Board / Elders | Evaluate high-level health for long-term vision. |

---

## 4. Vision Alignment
The Giving Insights Dashboard ensures that financial stewardship remains Transparent, Strategic, and Responsible.
