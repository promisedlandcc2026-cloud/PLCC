
# Event Planning Templates & Examples

These templates are designed to streamline the planning process in **Events > Plan**. 
Use them as a starting point and customize them to fit your specific event needs.

---

## 📅 Sunday Morning Service (Standard)
*A structured 90-minute service flow.*

### Run of Show
1.  **Volunteer Huddle** (15 mins) - Pre-service briefing & prayer.
2.  **Doors Open** (15 mins) - Welcome team in place, background music on.
3.  **Worship Set (Greeting)** (15 mins) - 2 upbeat songs + Prayer.
4.  **Welcome & Announcements** (5 mins) - Highlight key events.
5.  **Tithes & Offerings** (5 mins) - Brief exhortation & collection.
6.  **Worship Set (Main)** (15 mins) - 2-3 worship songs.
7.  **Sermon** (40 mins) - Message & teaching.
8.  **Altar Call / Response** (10 mins) - Prayer time.
9.  **Closing / Benediction** (5 mins) - Final blessing & dismissal.

---

## 📖 Midweek Bible Study
*A simpler, teaching-focused format.*

### Run of Show
1.  **Fellowship & Coffee** (15 mins) - Informal gathering.
2.  **Opening Prayer** (5 mins) - Focusing the group.
3.  **Worship (Acoustic)** (10 mins) - 1-2 simple songs.
4.  **Bible Study / Teaching** (45 mins) - In-depth study.
5.  **Small Group Discussion** (20 mins) - Breakout sessions.
6.  **Corporate Prayer** (10 mins) - Praying for needs.
7.  **Dismissal**

---

## 🔥 Leadership / Team Meeting
*Agenda-driven session for staff or volunteers.*

### Run of Show
1.  **Wins & Testimonies** (10 mins) - Celebrating recent successes.
2.  **Devotional** (10 mins) - Spiritual encouragement.
3.  **Review Previous Action Items** (10 mins) - Accountability.
4.  **Key Discussion Topic 1** (20 mins) - E.g., Upcoming event strategy.
5.  **Department Updates** (15 mins) - Brief reports from leads.
6.  **New Action Items** (5 mins) - Assign tasks & deadlines.
7.  **Closing Prayer** (5 mins)

---

## 🎉 Outreach Event / Picnic
*Logistics-heavy timeline.*

### Run of Show
1.  **Setup Crew Arrival** (60 mins) - Tents, tables, sound system.
2.  **Volunteer Check-in** (15 mins) - Assignment distribution.
3.  **Event Start / Welcome** (10 mins) - Generic announcement.
4.  **Activity Block 1** (45 mins) - Games / Food service begins.
5.  **Brief Gospel Presentation** (10 mins) - Short message.
6.  **Activity Block 2** (45 mins) - Continuation.
7.  **Cleanup Crew Start** (30 mins) - Begin breakdown.
8.  **Final Tear Down** (45 mins) - Load out.
